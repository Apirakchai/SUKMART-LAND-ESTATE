Overview

Kan Chu, a Prime Location
at the Heart of Bueng Sam Phan

A large tract in Phetchabun's agricultural economic zone, well connected to two of the country's leading agro-industrial operations, Golden Line Business and Saha Farms

A handsome rectangular plot with 250 metres of frontage on a 4-lane main road and 700 metres of depth, already filled and levelled

A plot of 110 rai 1 ngan 59 sq. wah in Kan Chu subdistrict, Bueng Sam Phan district, Phetchabun province, fronting public Highway 2275 (4 lanes) and just 2.5 kilometres from the Kan Chu SAO junction. Mains water and 3-phase electricity are already in place and ready to use. Ideally suited to large-scale agriculture, a farm, a factory or a real-estate development.

The plot currently has about 60 rai of mango trees and about 50 rai of eucalyptus, already established and still productive
