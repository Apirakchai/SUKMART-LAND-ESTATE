Overview

A Former Resort, Ready to Develop

A resort property of 9 rai 36 sq. wah in the Tha Muang area of Kanchanaburi province, close to the motorway and main roads. Well suited to investment in a tourism business.

Ready-to-use land, formerly a resort, with 10 bungalows, a large pond, concrete roads all around and water channels along the north and west sides

A plot of 9 rai 36 sq. wah in Nong Khao subdistrict, Tha Muang district, Kanchanaburi province. Formerly run as a resort, it has 10 bungalows and a 2.5-ngan (1,000 sq. m) pond. Water and electricity are connected and the land is filled.

A prime location just 1.5 kilometres from the M81 Kanchanaburi–Bang Yai motorway entrance and only 500 metres from Highway 324. Well suited to development as a resort, accommodation, a hotel or another tourism business.
