Terms

Co-Broking Terms

The same terms apply to every property. Details can be negotiated directly with the owner

01

3% commission

The broker commission is 3% of the sale price. All brokers are welcome, both individuals and companies

02

Paid on completion of transfer

The owner pays the commission once the sale is fully completed, that is, when ownership has been transferred and the purchase price received in full

03

Multiple brokers

Where sub-brokers or several parties work together, they are asked to agree and divide the commission among themselves

04

Sold as a whole

Every property is sold in its entirety. The owner does not wish to subdivide and sell in smaller parcels

05

Buyer's payment

Payment is accepted in cash, by bank transfer and in digital currency. Instalment payment directly to the owner is not offered

06

Site visits

Book in advance via Line or by phone on 081-713-2111. The original title documents can be viewed on the day of the visit
