Overview

A Large-Scale Farm

A Thai native-cross chicken farm (three-way cross) on 31 rai 2 ngan 50 sq. wah, with 12 poultry houses holding 5,500 birds each, for a total of 66,000 birds.

A large farm raising Thai native-cross chickens (three-way cross), whose creamy-white meat with a yellow tinge is attractive and appetising. Certification no. กษ 02-6901-71-100-070353 GAP

Farm 3 covers 31 rai 2 ngan 50 sq. wah at 22/27 Moo 8, Pak Phraek subdistrict, Mueang district, Kanchanaburi province. It has 12 poultry houses: 9 open-sided netted houses and 3 EVAP (evaporative-cooled) houses.

Each house can stock 5,500 birds, giving a maximum of 66,000 across the 12 houses. Estimated output is 12,000 day-old chicks per month. Thai chicken has creamy-white meat with a yellow tinge, attractive and appetising.
