Overview

A Fully Integrated Farm

A black chicken farm in operation since 2003, with breeder males and females, a hatchery, a day-old chick house and rearing houses all on one site. Excellent-quality black chicken meat, bones and offal.

A farm with fully integrated production, from breeder stock and hatching through to day-old chicks and market-ready birds. Official certification no. กษ 02-6901-71-100-070309 GAP

A farm of 8 rai 2 ngan 21.2 sq. wah at 88 Moo 1, Tha Makham subdistrict, Mueang district, Kanchanaburi province, run by Black Chicken Farm (Kanchanaburi) Co., Ltd., established in 2003 (Black Chicken Farm Since 2003)

It comprises 8 open-sided netted houses and 6 EVAP (evaporative-cooled) houses, 14 in all, with capacity for more than 36,700 black chickens or 24,350 Thai chickens/broilers, and produces about 23,000 day-old chicks per month.
